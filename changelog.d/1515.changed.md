- **Changelog entries are per-PR fragments, so parallel PRs stop conflicting on `CHANGELOG.md`** (#1515). Add `changelog.d/<issue>.<type>.md` (type: `added`, `changed`, `deprecated`, `removed`, `fixed` or `security`) holding the bullet lines instead of editing `## [Unreleased]`. `make release-bump` collects them into the new `## [x.y.z]` section, grouped under the right `###` heading and ordered by issue number, merges what is still under `[Unreleased]`, and deletes the collected files. `make changelog-preview` prints that section; `make release-check` refuses a malformed fragment name and a release section with no entries.

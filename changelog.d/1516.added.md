- **Isolated review checkouts** (#1516). `swarm_runtime.review_checkout` and `jury.run_gate(..., head_sha=...)` allocate unique, invocation-owned detached checkouts under `.keel/worktrees/review-*` (refusing on path collisions and warning if worktree removal fails), and the `ship` and `review-cycle` adapters instruct reviewer dispatches to use deterministic per-run `.keel/worktrees/review-*` checkouts cleaned up via `keel worktree-remove` after completion.
- **Behaviour change — `keel ship` `jury` gate runs in a detached checkout** (#1516). When `keel ship` runs the `jury` gate, it now executes `jury` in a fresh detached checkout of the reviewed commit (`--head-sha` or `HEAD`), copying untracked `jury.toml`, `jury-policy.toml`, and `.jury/` from the repository root when present; uncommitted working-tree edits and other untracked directories (e.g. `.keel/state`) are not visible to the panel.
- **Staged contamination warnings on `keel ship` and `keel wrap`** (#1516). `keel ship` and `keel wrap` accept `--implementer-file` (with `keel ship` also falling back to `--declared-file`) and `--since` (a committed-history baseline; **behaviour change**: `keel wrap --since` now queries git history when `--implementer-file` is omitted, warning when `--since` is unresolvable or has no commits) to warn on stderr and in the JSON result when staged modified or deleted files (`git diff --cached`) fall outside the baseline.

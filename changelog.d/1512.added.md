- **`keel stale-blockers` lists open PRs whose stated blocker has cleared** (#1512). A PR body that says `blocked by #N`, `depends on #N` or `do not merge before #N|<tag>` is checked against GitHub. `/keel:stale-prs` and `/keel:morning` run it and surface the result.
  - A blocker is `cleared` when its PR merged, its issue closed as completed, or its tag exists; a PR closed unmerged or an issue closed as not planned or a duplicate is `abandoned` (reported, not counted as cleared).
  - The parser reads the recognised phrases (`blocked by`, `depends on`, `waiting on`, `blocked until`, `dependency:` / `needs dependency:`, and `do not merge before|until|prior to`) and marks a PR incomplete when the captured blocker spans do not match the reference-like token spans in a phrase's zone (the rest of its line, or the immediately following non-blank line when the phrase ends its line, plus any contiguous bullet lines directly below that). Because the zone covers the whole line and any immediately following bullets, an unrelated reference on the same line (`Depends on #12. Closes #15`) or on a contiguous bullet line without a blank line before it (`Depends on #12` followed by `- Fixes #15`) also marks the PR `incomplete: true` (an expected false negative for `all_cleared`; the PR is still listed for inspection). `all_cleared` is a best-effort hint for an operator to inspect, never a certificate: an operator must read the PR before acting. What is ignored: standard blockquotes (`> ...`), collapsed sections (`<details>...</details>`, including an unterminated `<details>` to the end of the body), negated/historical phrases (`not blocked by`, `previously blocked by`), phrases with no reference-like token at all (`behaviour depends on the config`, `Dependency: bump`), phrases or headings separated from their targets by a blank line (`Blocked by\n\n#5`, `## Blocked by\n\n- #5`), and unrecognised phrasings (`blocked on`, `waiting for`, `stacked on`, `requires`). Author alert blocks (`> [!NOTE]`, `> [!WARNING]`, etc.) are author claims and are scanned. A PR whose references could not be read is listed (`blockers: []`, `incomplete: true`) rather than dropped, and a `#N` link into another repository or a mismatched target (`[#12](#99)`) is treated as foreign and not resolved here.
  - A failed read is reported: a blocker that cannot be read is `unknown` (with a reason), its PR is listed, the output counts them, and the command exits 2 (exit 0 means every blocker on the scanned PRs was read, including when `truncated: true` indicates `--limit` capped the open-PR list).
